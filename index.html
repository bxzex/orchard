The orchard

We let the fruit decide how the cider tastes.

Forty acres, eleven varieties, and trees older than anyone who works here. We pick by hand, press in the barn, and ferment on wild yeast in old oak. Some years that makes a sharper cider, some years a softer one. We bottle whatever the orchard gives us.
